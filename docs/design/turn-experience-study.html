SUNMARK / INTERACTION STUDY 01

A turn that
feels like play.

Touch an object. See its consequence. Keep the island in view.

  1. Tap the dice and follow the result into your hand.
  2. Open Build. Pin a settlement and inspect its missing wool.
  3. Trade four wood for wool, then preview and build.
  4. Explore your cards, or restart with a seven.
Explore the study

Independent interaction prototype. Scripted resources, locations, cards, and opponents; no real match or save is changed. The real game retains its Phaser renderer and authoritative rules.
